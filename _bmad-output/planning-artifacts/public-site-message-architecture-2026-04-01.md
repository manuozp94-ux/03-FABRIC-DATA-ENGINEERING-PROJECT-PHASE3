# Public-Site Message Architecture

Date: 2026-04-01
Status: active guidance

## Canonical Product Statement

A governed supply-chain decision-support proof built from real operational logic and presented through a web-first public-safe experience.

## Primary Audience

Hiring teams and technical reviewers who want to evaluate analytical judgment, delivery discipline, and bounded Microsoft Fabric proof in one coherent case.

## Secondary Audience

Consulting-style conversations where fragmented operational reporting still needs structure, trust, and a credible technical path.

## Core Messages

1. This is a bounded decision-support case, not an ERP replacement or full planning suite.
2. The website is the main product surface; Fabric is bounded technical proof that supports it.
3. The project values governed logic, publication safety, and traceable refresh discipline over UI-first iteration.
4. The current proof is technically inspectable and intentionally honest about what remains deferred.

## Route Jobs

### Landing

- State the operating problem quickly
- Position the product clearly
- Give the reader a simple route choice

### Case Study

- Show the business decision and why it matters
- Make the stakes visible before the metrics
- Keep the read business-first, not tool-first

### Technical Method

- Explain the bounded architecture and proof path
- Show why the proof is credible
- Keep the tone rigorous without becoming a documentation dump

## Copy Rules

- Prefer confident explanation over defensive explanation.
- Keep the product affirmative before listing exclusions.
- Use technical detail to support trust, not to dominate the message.
- Keep Fabric subordinate to the product narrative.
