# Internal Planning Notes

Date: 2026-03-24
Purpose: Preserve Party Mode conclusions that should inform later positioning, scope, and go-to-market decisions but do not belong directly inside the Target Users section of the product brief.

## Positioning Decisions

- The public portfolio should position the project first as proof of professional transformation capability, not as a content channel.
- Educational content should operate as a discovery and trust-building channel, not as the product itself.
- The public message should avoid framing the creator as a generic BI content creator or dashboard specialist.
- The strongest public narrative is: from fragmented operational data and siloed business logic to a governed, Fabric-native, decision-oriented analytics platform.

## Audience Balance

- The portfolio can support both employment and consulting outcomes, but the layers should not compete on the same surface.
- A yin-yang structure is appropriate:
  - Primary presentation layer: employability and professional credibility.
  - Secondary trust layer: business use cases and applied scenarios that also serve prospective consulting clients.
- Case studies or business use cases are a better bridge asset than generic tutorials or internal-style user stories.

## Internal Capability Narrative

- The project should internally track the creator's growing autonomy in SQL, Python, Spark, and Fabric implementation.
- That autonomy narrative should be developed as if it could be made visible, but should remain outside the public portfolio unless later needed in a curated form.
- Public positioning should avoid overstating full autonomous execution if that is not yet consistently true.
- The recommended internal framing is: high business and platform judgment, strong operational context, and increasing hands-on execution supported by disciplined evidence.

## Delivery Guidance

- Structure the project in layers so each cycle increases real autonomy instead of hiding dependencies.
- Track what was designed independently, what was implemented with assistance, and what can now be repeated with less support.
- Use small, verifiable deliverables and frequent design-review-validation loops.
- Future scope, architecture, and go-to-market documentation should reuse these notes where relevant.

## Learning Backlog

- Add `openpyxl` to the internal learning backlog as a spreadsheet-processing tool to understand and use confidently for workbook inspection, schema discovery, and controlled transformation support.
- Maintain an inventory of open technical and business questions encountered during the project so they can be converted into study tasks, resolved understanding, and reusable documentation.
- Treat the study backlog as an internal autonomy-building asset, not part of the public portfolio surface.
