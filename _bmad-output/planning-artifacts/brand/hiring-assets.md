## Interview Talking Points

### What problem does the portfolio solve?

It addresses the challenge of proving real analytics and platform capability in public when the most meaningful source work exists inside confidential business environments and fragmented historical artifacts.

### Why Microsoft Fabric?

Fabric is being used as the primary end-to-end platform for ingestion, transformation, semantic readiness, and analytical presentation. The goal is to show platform coherence, not only isolated tooling knowledge.

### What is the MVP trying to prove?

That a limited but realistic operational slice can be turned into a governed, publishable analytical product that supports cross-functional reasoning across demand, inventory, logistics, and business-value proxy analysis.

### Why use masking and abstraction instead of raw public datasets?

Because the project is grounded in real business experience, but it must remain safe to publish. The abstraction layer preserves business behavior without exposing company identity or proprietary logic.

### What makes the project non-generic?

It is based on real-world operational complexity, historical artifact consolidation, derived dimensions, controlled realism, and a structured approach to turning fragmented logic into a modern analytical product.
