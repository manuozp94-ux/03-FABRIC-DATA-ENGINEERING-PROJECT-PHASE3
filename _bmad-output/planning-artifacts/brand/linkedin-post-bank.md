# LinkedIn Post Bank

Date: 2026-03-25
Purpose: Maintain a reusable bank of LinkedIn post concepts, hooks, structures, and CTAs tied directly to the portfolio, case studies, and brand strategy.

## Reusable Post Formula

Use this structure by default:
- Hook: surprising or specific insight
- Context: what problem or pattern was observed
- Point of view: what the creator believes or learned
- Proof: brief project or architecture reference
- CTA: invite portfolio review, conversation, or follow-up

## CTA Library for LinkedIn

Use one CTA per post:
- Review the case study
- Follow the build journey
- Connect if your team is facing similar fragmentation
- Message me if you want to compare approaches
- See how this portfolio models the problem

## Posting Guidance

- Do not write every post like a lesson.
- Rotate between expertise, narrative, business value, and build process.
- Keep at least one post out of every four directly tied to a concrete portfolio artifact.
- Use the comments to add technical detail if the main post is more commercial.
