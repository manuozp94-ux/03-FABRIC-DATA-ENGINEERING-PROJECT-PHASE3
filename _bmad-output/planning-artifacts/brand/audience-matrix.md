# Audience Matrix

Date: 2026-03-25
Purpose: Define the core audience segments, their motivations, the message each one needs to hear, and the most effective content and conversion path for each segment.

## Audience Segments

| Audience | Primary Need | What They Need to Believe | Most Effective Message | Best Content Types | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recruiter | Fast role fit evaluation | This portfolio is credible, role-relevant, and worth advancing | Applied Microsoft Fabric and analytics engineering capability grounded in real business complexity | LinkedIn posts, recruiter summary, CV project blurbs, portfolio landing page | Review portfolio and schedule screening |
| Hiring Manager | Deeper capability validation | The creator can connect business context, architecture, and analytical delivery | End-to-end transformation from fragmented operational data to governed analytical products | Case study page, architecture summary, build walkthrough, hiring asset brief | Move to interview or technical review |
| Consulting Prospect | Problem-to-solution confidence | This person can help modernize siloed operational analytics environments | Fabric-native transformation with operational and commercial reasoning | Case study summary, commercial script, consulting one-pager, LinkedIn value post | Book discovery call |
| Analytics Sponsor | Internal change feasibility | This approach can realistically map to a growing organization with fragmented logic | Structured path from fragmented reporting and spreadsheet logic to a governed analytical stack | Applied scenarios, future-state explanation, supporting cross-functional views | Continue architecture conversation |
| Peer / Practitioner | Technical trust | The creator understands modern architecture and can explain it clearly | Thoughtful, structured, AI-assisted analytics engineering work | Technical posts, short tutorials, long-form walkthroughs | Follow, share, refer |
| Community Learner | Accessible learning path | The work is advanced but explainable and worth following | Clear explanations of Fabric, masking, dimensions, and portfolio thinking | Short tutorials, carousel posts, video explainers | Follow and consume more content |

## Messaging Priorities by Audience

### Recruiter

Priority messages:
- Microsoft Fabric portfolio with clear role relevance
- Real operational complexity, not toy data
- Cross-functional analytics experience across sales, logistics, supply chain, and finance

Avoid:
- too much jargon
- long implementation detail before establishing role fit

### Hiring Manager

Priority messages:
- strong judgment in scope, modeling, and abstraction
- clear thinking about messy enterprise realities
- credible end-to-end architecture and analytical delivery

Avoid:
- content-creator framing
- generic BI claims without modeled proof

### Consulting Prospect

Priority messages:
- understands siloed data realities and fragmented business logic
- can package messy analytics work into a modern governed architecture
- can connect operational problems to economic value discussion

Avoid:
- overemphasis on job-seeking narrative
- overly internal or resume-style framing

### Peer / Practitioner

Priority messages:
- structured AI-assisted workflow
- Fabric-native analytical design
- derived dimensions, masking, and controlled realism

Avoid:
- over-polished marketing language without technical substance

## Conversion Hierarchy

### Hiring Conversion Path

1. Discover a post or profile snippet
2. Visit portfolio or hiring asset summary
3. Understand role fit quickly
4. Review case-study proof
5. Move to screening or interview

### Consulting Conversion Path

1. Discover a value-oriented post or short commercial
2. Visit case study or consulting summary
3. Recognize a familiar business problem
4. See a credible transformation path
5. Book discovery call

### Authority Conversion Path

1. Discover a technical or educational post
2. Consume a short tutorial or walkthrough
3. Associate the creator with clarity and depth
4. Follow, share, or refer
5. Potentially convert later into hiring or consulting lead

## Practical Content Matching Rules

- If the audience is `Recruiter`, lead with role clarity and portfolio proof.
- If the audience is `Hiring Manager`, lead with architecture and applied judgment.
- If the audience is `Consulting Prospect`, lead with transformation and business value.
- If the audience is `Peer / Practitioner`, lead with insight and implementation reasoning.
- If the audience is `Learner`, lead with concept explanation and practical takeaways.

## Working Rule

Every public asset should be tagged internally with:
- target audience
- content pillar
- desired CTA
- source artifact reused

If those four fields are not clear, the asset is probably not focused enough.
