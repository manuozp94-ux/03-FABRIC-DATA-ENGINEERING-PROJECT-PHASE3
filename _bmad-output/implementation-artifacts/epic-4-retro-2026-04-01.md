# Epic 4 Retrospective: Turn the Technical Method into Bounded Proof

Date: 2026-04-01
Epic Status: done
Retrospective Status: done

## Epic Review

### Intended Outcome

Epic 4 was meant to prove that the project had real technical discipline behind the web experience: a bounded source slice, explicit masking and evidence boundaries, model and grain explanation, and one Fabric or Power BI proof surface that stayed subordinate to the site.

### What We Completed

- Story 4.1 explained the approved source slice and the three-zone boundary across private preparation, governed analytics, and public delivery.
- Story 4.2 documented masking, abstraction, simplifications, and public-safe treatment categories.
- Story 4.3 tied modeled entities and grain back to business questions instead of leaving the model as generic schema talk.
- Story 4.4 assembled the bounded technical evidence bundle and labeled direct, indirect, and deferred proof coverage.
- Story 4.5 formalized the public-evidence boundary and made exclusions explicit instead of implied.
- Story 4.6 created the first bounded Fabric proof path with Lakehouse, semantic model, report shell, and technical-method embed support.

### What Went Well

- The technical route stopped being abstract. It became a real explanation layer with source scope, model logic, validation posture, and bounded platform evidence.
- Boundary discipline held up under pressure. The project proved that it could show real technical rigor without collapsing into a raw repo tour or exposing private-prep leverage.
- The Fabric proof path became concrete. The Lakehouse, semantic model, report shell, and embed path all moved from concept to visible bounded proof.
- Review kept improving the right things. The strongest gains came from clarifying what the proof actually demonstrated and what remained intentionally deferred.

### What Was Harder Than Expected

- It was harder than expected to explain technical rigor without sounding either evasive or overly tool-centric.
- The term "Bronze" created confusion because the project had two valid meanings in play: messy upstream realism and the proof-side landed copy of governed data.
- The first Fabric proof surfaced a useful tension: the platform path was real, but the business logic underneath it was still too provisional to justify heavy UI iteration.
- Technical credibility required more than assets. It required careful wording about what was live, what was bounded, and what was not yet complete.

### Key Discoveries

- Technical proof works best when it reinforces the product thesis rather than trying to become a second product.
- The public-safe boundary is more persuasive when exclusions are named explicitly instead of quietly omitted.
- A bounded Fabric proof shell is valuable early, but only if it is treated as proof of architecture and handoff discipline, not as the place to discover unstable business logic.
- Controlled realism needed its own clearer framing. Without that, the project risked mixing source messiness, public-safe data, and proof Bronze into one blurry story.

### Review Patterns

- The most useful review feedback challenged implied claims, especially around proof coverage, live-vs-bounded wording, and whether a term suggested more completeness than the repo actually had.
- Boundary wording mattered as much as code. Several improvements came from tightening narrative distinctions rather than from changing the technical mechanism itself.
- The project improved whenever we asked whether the technical route was proving disciplined delivery or merely listing assets.

### Technical Debt and Open Risks

- The technical-method route became much stronger, but it now carries more narrative responsibility and will need occasional wording maintenance as the data path evolves.
- The controlled-realism story was only partially operationalized inside Epic 4 and had to be clarified later in Epic 5.
- The Fabric proof shell existed, but its underlying data still reflected a smoke-scale market at the end of the epic.
- Desktop-authored visual polish remained correctly deferred, but that means the proof experience still depends on disciplined explanation more than on final report ergonomics.

## Next Epic Preparation

### Epic 5 Readiness

Epic 5 was ready because Epic 4 proved the platform path and the public technical boundary. The next logical step was no longer "can we show Fabric?" but "can we make the data behave like a believable business before we keep polishing reports?"

### What Epic 5 Needed to Preserve

- Keep the web route primary and the Fabric proof subordinate.
- Preserve the three-zone architecture and the explicit evidence boundary.
- Avoid using manual report iteration to compensate for unstable serving logic.
- Keep the proof honest about what is representative and what is still seed-scale.

### Action Items

- Owner: Product direction
  Keep the logic-first principle explicit so report composition never outruns data credibility.

- Owner: Data generation design
  Turn controlled realism, business rules, and inventory-loop behavior into executable controls rather than leaving them as narrative intent.

- Owner: Technical method
  Continue tightening language when terms like Bronze, proof, current, candidate, and approved package carry different meanings in different layers.

- Owner: Delivery discipline
  Keep review focused on overclaim risk, boundary drift, and whether the technical route stays aligned with the actual live proof state.

### Carry-Forward Agreements

- Fabric proof remains bounded and governed.
- Controlled realism belongs upstream and must be explained separately from the proof-side medallion path.
- Manual Power BI polish remains deferred until serving logic is stable enough to avoid churn.
- The technical-method route should describe what is true today, not what might be true later.

## Closing Summary

Epic 4 succeeded because it turned technical credibility into something inspectable without breaking the public-safe boundary. The biggest win was not only the live Fabric proof shell, but the fact that the project learned how to explain architecture, evidence, and limits honestly. That set up Epic 5 to focus on logic quality instead of platform theater.
