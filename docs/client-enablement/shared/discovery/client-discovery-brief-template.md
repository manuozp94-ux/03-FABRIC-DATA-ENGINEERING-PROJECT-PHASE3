# Client Discovery Brief Template

Status: Template
Purpose: Capture the smallest complete discovery picture before any architecture, modeling, or delivery work begins.

## Usage

Use this template at the start of a client engagement.

The goal is not to describe every possible requirement. The goal is to lock:

- the business question
- the decision-makers
- the key metrics
- the source reality
- the sensitivity and privacy boundary
- the first useful deliverable
