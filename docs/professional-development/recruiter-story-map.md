# Recruiter Story Map

Status: active interview and recruiter-facing reference
Last updated: 2026-04-14

## One-Line Positioning

Analytics engineer and BI lead who turns fragmented operational logic into governed
decision-support systems across sales, supply chain, logistics, pricing, and finance.

## Short Narrative

My background started close to operations: production, warehouse work, quality checks,
supply chain support, customer service, collections, and sales reporting.

That foundation shaped how I approach data. I do not start with dashboards first. I
start by asking how work actually moves, where decisions get blocked, and which rules
need to become reusable.

This portfolio formalizes that pattern as a bounded Fabric proof. The project models
a public-safe operating loop where demand pressure creates supply-chain and logistics
decisions, those decisions become orders or movements, and inventory state changes
feed governed analytical outputs.

## What The Project Proves

- Business framing: one operating problem is translated into clear decision views.
- Analytics engineering: source grains, facts, dimensions, rules, and validations are explicit.
- BI leadership: dashboard views are downstream of stable business logic.
- Fabric readiness: Bronze, Silver, Gold, semantic model, runtime governance, and report shell are tied together.
- Communication: the public site explains the proof without exposing private business material.

## Interview Sound Bites

- "The project is operating-loop first and platform second."
- "The dashboard is not the product; the governed decision logic is the product."
- "I separate recommendations from executed events so the model does not pretend every analytical signal became operational action."
- "Power BI is gated on purpose because this proof treats access and publication as governance concerns."
- "The portfolio is bounded, but the method is transferable to real Azure, SQL, Power BI, and Fabric modernization work."

## Claims To Keep Careful

Safe claim:

- built a bounded public-safe Fabric proof with governed contracts, validation, semantic handoff, and dashboard-ready outputs

Avoid:

- full enterprise Fabric ownership
- live operational control tower
- raw invoice-source finance
- anonymous public BI portal
- production ERP replacement
